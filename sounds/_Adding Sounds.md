# Animal Sounds — Download Guide

All sounds are **CC0 (no copyright)** from [freesound.org](https://freesound.org).

## How to download

1. Click a link below — it opens the exact sound on Freesound
2. Click the **Download** button (free account required — sign up once)
3. Rename the downloaded file exactly as shown in the **Save as** column
4. Put it in this `sounds/` folder

The site works without any sounds — missing sounds are silently skipped.

## Tips

- Pick sounds that are **2–8 seconds** long — short enough not to distract
- Avoid clips with music, commentary or background noise
- Some searches (scorpion, chameleon, jellyfish, squid) may have few results — skip those, the app works fine without them
- After downloading, if the file is `.ogg` or `.wav`, rename it to `.mp3` — most browsers handle all three formats
